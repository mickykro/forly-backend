# 50 Prominent Facebook Groups for Real-Estate Marketing, Sales, and Rentals in Israel

**Prepared by Manus AI**  
**Research date:** 23 August 2026 (GMT+3)

## What this list represents

This directory identifies **50 prominent Facebook Groups used to market, find, rent, buy, or sell residential property in Israel**. It is intended as a practical media and lead-generation resource, rather than as an official Facebook ranking. Facebook does not publish a complete national Group ranking, membership changes continuously, and many Groups are private or restrict content visibility.

Entries **1–38** come from a May 2026 directory of Israel’s largest rental Groups, organized by geography and reported with rounded membership figures. Entries **39–50** are additional city and English-language housing Groups listed in Nefesh B’Nefesh’s housing-resource guide, last updated in April 2026. [1] [2] The order below is therefore by **market area**, not by an asserted national 1–50 membership rank. Every title links directly to its Facebook Group; the final quality check confirmed **50 unique group links**.

> **Use before posting:** Read the live Group rules first. “No broker,” “owner only,” roommate, student, or sublet Groups may not permit agent listings, duplicated posts, or sales advertising.

## Selection guidance

For a **property sale**, start with the Groups explicitly covering sales and rentals: rows 8, 13, 21, 27, 29, 36–38, and 44–46. For **rentals**, a city-specific audience is usually more useful than an unrelated national audience; the 2026 directory specifically observes that local relevance can be more valuable than raw Group size. [1] For English-speaking prospective tenants or buyers, rows 39–48 are useful complementary channels. In all cases, match the listing’s language to the audience and avoid reposting at a frequency forbidden by the Group rules.
