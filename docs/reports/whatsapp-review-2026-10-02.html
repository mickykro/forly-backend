Bottom line

One real bug since the last check, and it is a new way of hitting an old failure mode: the listing-intake opener fires on any incoming message that contains a link, with no check for what kind of message it is. On 2026-10-01 an outbound video to נופר הרוש שמאות מקרקעין ונדל״ן (972546582548) bounced her own WhatsApp Business away-message back at us — "תודה שפניתם לנופר הרוש... כרגע איני זמינה... כרטיס הביקור הדיגיטלי שלי: https://digitalcard1.co.il/nofar" — and because it carries both a link and real-estate-sounding words about her own business, the bot read it as a pasted listing ad and opened a brand-new property draft on top of her existing five: "קראתי את המודעה אבל לא מצאתי בה פרטים ברורים. נשלים ביחד. באיזו עיר הנכס?", then "מה המחיר?" when she only reacted with a ❤️. She never asked to create anything in this exchange.

Fixed in server/property-draft.js: openerKind() now recognizes a short set of away-message / "thanks for contacting us" phrasings common to Israeli WhatsApp Business auto-replies ("תודה ש(יצרת קשר/פנית...)", "אינ(י/נו) זמינ...", "נחזור אלי(ך/כם)", "נשיב ברגע שנחזור", "קיבלנו את הודעתכם/פנייתך") and bails out before the link/keyword/listing-text checks run, so none of them can fire on an auto-reply — a message like this now falls through to ordinary chat instead of opening a draft. This is the same class of noise flagged for the lead-outreach sequence in earlier reviews (a business's own auto-responder bouncing back at an outbound send), but this is the first time it has been shown to actually corrupt a real agent's flow rather than just repeat a canned follow-up, so it is fixed here rather than filed as a watch item.

Everything else flagged in the last several reviews held up: no stale numbered-menu repeats, no trycloudflare.com leaks in the new-evidence window, the per-property currency field and the numbered/worded confirmation fix from 2026-10-01 both held on every draft touched since. No business decision is needed this cycle. One n8n item is still open — the photo-batch "one text + one image per photo" progress spam — but it is unchanged from the last four reviews and was already sent to the operator on 2026-09-29/30, so no new WhatsApp notification went out for it today.

Fixed A business's own away-message was read as a pasted listing and opened an unwanted draft

נופר הרוש שמאות מקרקעין ונדל״ן, 972546582548 — Oct 1, 18:33–18:35 (Israel time)

A demo/marketing video went out to this agent at 18:33:09. Sixteen seconds later her number sent back the auto-reply her own WhatsApp Business account is configured to send anyone who messages her:

תודה שפניתם לנופר הרוש – שמאות מקרקעין ונדל״ן. 🌸

באיזו עיר הנכס?
[reaction ❤️] [reaction ❤]
מה המחיר? (למשל 2,900,000 או 2.9 מיליון; לשכירות — לחודש)

This agent already has five live property pages and had used the bot correctly minutes earlier the same day ("היי אני צריכה לעדכן מחיר את יכולה לעשות את זה?" → the bot correctly listed all five edit links). The away-message text has nothing to do with a listing — it is her own greeting, with a digital-business-card link and two words that happen to read as real-estate-flavored ("מקרקעין ונדל״ן" is literally her job title). property-draft.js's openerKind() ran findUrl() first and matched the digital-card link, so it opened a "link" draft regardless of what the surrounding text said; a longer message with two matching words would have hit looksLikeListing() the same way even without a link. Nothing in the opener path distinguished "a listing to parse" from "an unrelated message that happens to contain a link."

Fix: openerKind() now checks first for the handful of fixed phrasings that Israeli WhatsApp Business away-messages and "thanks for contacting us" auto-replies use, and returns null immediately when one matches — before the link, keyword, or listing-text checks get a chance to fire. A real listing paste that happens to open with "תודה..." for some other reason is not affected; the guard is phrase-specific (away/receipt wording), not a generic "starts with תודה" cutoff.

A regression test replays this exact message, plus two other auto-reply shapes seen elsewhere in this review's sample (קארין חביב's "תודה שיצרת קשר עם..." greeting and a plain "איננו זמינים כעת... נשיב ברגע שנחזור" away-note), in server/property-draft.test.js.
