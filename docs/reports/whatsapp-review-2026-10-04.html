Found and fixed

Fixed "סגור" fell through to n8n's generic fallback instead of stopping photo edits

‏צח בן בסט, 972547221770 — Sep 29, 18:16–18:17

Right after cancelling an open draft, this agent typed a one-word סגור — a perfectly natural way to say "stop"/"close" in Hebrew:

ביטול
ביטלתי את הטיוטה. אפשר להתחיל מחדש עם קישור, טקסט או ״נכס חדש״.
סגור
לא בטוחה למה התכוונת 🙂 אפשר לכתוב במילים מה לעשות?

property-draft.js's STOP_RE already recognized עצור, די, מספיק, תפסיק/י and "אל תערוך/תמשיך..." as commands that stop n8n's photo-edit loop (server/whatsapp-intake.js checks this before anything else, independent of whether a draft is open). סגור and its conjugations weren't in that list, so the message never matched, this backend reported handled: false, and n8n's own catch-all answered with its off-brand "not sure what you meant" line instead of actually stopping anything.

Fix: added סגור, תסגור, תסגרי to STOP_RE as stop-synonyms — kept distinct from בטל/ביטול (already a separate command: cancel the whole draft, not just the running edits), so the two don't collide. A regression test replays this exact exchange in server/whatsapp-flows.test.js, next to the existing עצור/די stop tests.

fix(property-draft): "סגור" also stops photo edits, like עצור and די

Worth watching

Watch A reply sent while a page or video is building can be silently dropped

Code-read finding, not caught live this cycle — flagging for the next review to confirm against fresh traffic before touching it

While tracing the numbered-menu repeats reported in earlier cycles, the draft.status === "building" branch in server/whatsapp-intake.js turned out to return not_ours for any reply that isn't a recognized new-property opener — which would silently drop a numbered menu reply (e.g. a design choice, or "1"/"2" to a just-sent offer) typed in the gap while a page or video is being built, handing it to n8n's own bot instead, which has no idea a menu is pending. No transcript this cycle happens to land in that exact window, so this is not confirmed as the cause of any specific conversation yet — it's a latent gap found by reading the code, not a replayed bug. Given how many narrow timing fixes already exist in this exact file, it's flagged for confirmation rather than changed without a reproducing case.
