Bottom line

One real, reproducible bug this cycle, and it's a money one: an agent pasted a listing for a villa in Crete, Greece priced "285,000 אירו" (euro) — the extractor always assumed shekels, so the draft (and nearly the published page) showed ₪285,000, about a quarter of the actual asking price. Root cause was a hard "price in ILS" instruction in server/listing-extract.js's prompt with no currency awareness at all. Properties now carry their own currency (₪/€/$): the bot keeps the number as given and records the currency it names — in a pasted listing, a direct answer, or a correction — and the page editor has a currency selector. Every public page design, share preview and portfolio card shows the property's own currency; existing pages stay ₪.

Everything flagged in the last two reviews (2026-09-29, 2026-09-30 08:50) stayed fixed: no trycloudflare.com link leaks, and the "1"/"2" ignored after a photo batch didn't recur in any post-fix conversation. Two items stay open outside this repo: the photo-batch caption spam (still unresolved, third report in a row), and the competing n8n handler on the "build a page from these photos?" offer — now confirmed to also mis-fire on the accept path, not just decline, which is new evidence worth flagging with more urgency. One new business question came up: a real customer is already marketing an overseas property in euros through Forly, and the product has no notion of a non-ILS listing today.

Found and fixed

Fixed A euro price silently became a shekel price — off by roughly 4× → properties now have a currency

Gal / "create vision", 972526003708 — Sep 30, 10:47–11:05

The agent sent 7 photos of an old house plus a long free-text description, then when asked "which city?" pasted three marketing posts for a villa in Walichada, Crete (Greece), stating המחיר הוא 285,000 אירו three times over. The extractor pulled the number out fine — and relabeled it in shekels:

…המחיר הוא 285,000 אירו… (×3, across three pasted posts)
עדכנתי: שכונה וליכאדה, סוג עסקה למכירה, מחיר ₪285,000, מספר חדרים 3, שטח במ״ר 400, חניה אחת ✅
וזה לא 285,000 שח, אלא € יורו
לא הצלחתי להבין את הקומה. באיזו קומה? (correction not understood — bot was asking about floor and stayed there)

listing-extract.js's system prompt told the model, unconditionally, "price in ILS" — with no branch for a stated foreign currency, so a Claude-Haiku extraction call that is otherwise honest about "never guess" had no way to say "this isn't shekels." A live property listing was about to go up at roughly a quarter of its real asking price. The agent's own correction ("it's not 285,000 shekels, it's euro") didn't help either: it doesn't hit any of draft-corrections.js's price-related keywords, so it fell through to whatever field was currently being asked (floor) and got rejected as an invalid floor answer.

A property now has a currency (ILS/EUR/USD; missing = ILS, so every existing page is unchanged). Nothing is converted — the number is stored as the agent gave it, in the currency they named:

  • Chat: the currency named in the text is detected in code (server/currency.js), not left to the model. A pasted listing, a direct answer to "מה המחיר?" ("285,000 יורו"), a slash correction (/מטבע €) or a mid-flow correction like the agent's "וזה לא 285,000 שח, אלא € יורו" all set the property's currency — the last one now answers "עדכנתי: מטבע €" and goes back to the open question instead of "לא הצלחתי להבין את הקומה". Updating a live page from chat ("המחיר ביורו") changes that page's currency too.
  • Page editor: a ₪/€/$ selector next to the price; switching it also updates the price box on the page when it still shows the auto-formatted price.
  • Display: all 8 page designs, the share preview (OG), group-share text, the agent portfolio and the portal feed use the property's currency. Budget-based recommendations skip non-₪ pages, since visitor budgets are asked in ₪.

Tests replay this exact conversation through the chat handler (pasted listing, correction, direct answer), and all 8 designs were rendered in a browser with a euro page (€285,000, €2,850/m², no ₪) and with an old page (still ₪).
