n8n Photo-batch progress messages: one text + one image per photo

Seen in 4 of the sampled chats, e.g. Noy Harush & Tal Eynan (Bar Ilan) — 8-photo batches today and Sep 27

Every photo in a batch gets its own status line repeating the same long fixed sentence, plus the edited image — 16 outbound messages for an 8-photo batch:

עובדת על תמונה 1 מתוך 8 — שפר את התמונה: תאורה טובה וטבעית, חדות גבוהה, הסרת אלמנטים מיותרים/עומס, מראה נקי ומזמין שמושך לפנות ולשאול על הנכס
[image]
… repeats 8 times, identical caption each time

This text isn't anywhere in this repo — it's n8n's photo-edit workflow output, not this backend. Worth collapsing to one "working on your N photos ✨" message up front, then just the images as they land: roughly halves message volume and cuts exposure to WhatsApp/Green API rate limits (this review's own history-reading hit Green API's 429 throttling at a much lower request rate than a 12-photo burst sends at).
